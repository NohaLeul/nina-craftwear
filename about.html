A little about me

Made with heart, one stitch at a time.

Welcome to Craftwear by Nina — a creative journey shaped by curiosity, patience and handmade craftsmanship.

My Story

The beginning of Craftwear by Nina.

Eight years ago, I discovered something that would eventually become a very special part of my life. It all started when my sister sent me a video tutorial of someone making a crochet scarf. At the time, I knew very little about crochet and had never tried it before. I was looking for something creative to occupy my spare time, and the tutorial sparked my curiosity. I decided to give it a try, bought my first crochet supplies, and began making my very first piece.

That first creation was a scarf. To my surprise, it turned out beautifully, and I decided to gift it to my sister. What started as a simple experiment quickly grew into a genuine interest in crochet. I found joy in creating things with my own hands and wanted to keep exploring what I could make.

As time went on, I began making more pieces, especially clothing for children and infants. I created items for my own children and shared my work with close friends and relatives, often gifting handmade pieces to celebrate the arrival of their newborns.

With every new creation, I became more passionate about the craft. What had started as a hobby gradually became something I could imagine growing into something bigger. After seven years of creating, learning and sharing my work, I decided to take the next step and turn my passion into a business.

Choosing a name for the business was another special part of the journey. My real name is Netsanet, but I wanted a name that felt more familiar and personal. My closest friends and relatives had always called me Nina, so I thought it would be meaningful to use the name they knew me by. When I shared this idea with my younger sister, she suggested building the business name around Nina and creating a social media presence for it. With her help, my Instagram account was created, and she began managing it, helping me take my first steps into sharing my crochet creations with a wider audience. That's how the name Craftwear by Nina came to life.

One year ago, I officially began turning what had been a personal passion into a business, sharing my handmade crochet creations with more people. Today, what began with one simple scarf has grown into a journey built on creativity, patience, dedication and a love for handmade fashion. Every piece I create carries a little of that journey, and I hope each one brings as much joy to the person wearing it as it brings me to make it.

Craftwear by Nina — Made by hand. Worn with love.

How to Order

Browse the Shop and add the pieces you are interested in to your cart. Choose “Place Order” to prepare an email request, review the details and send it from your email app. I will then confirm availability and discuss payment and delivery arrangements with you directly. Your order is not confirmed and no payment is collected by this website until the details have been discussed directly.

Most pieces are made to order. Production generally takes a minimum of 3 days and up to 7 days, depending on the type of product. I will confirm the expected timeframe for your particular order when we discuss it.
