Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Craftwear by Nina handles information when you browse this website, contact us or prepare an order request.

1. Information you provide

If you contact us or prepare an order email, you may enter your name, phone number, email address, delivery address and message or order notes. These details are placed into an email draft on your device when you choose to continue. This website does not itself transmit that email; you decide whether to send it.

2. How information is used

Information you choose to send may be used to respond to enquiries, discuss product availability, coordinate an order and communicate about a request.

3. Local storage and cookies

This website uses browser local storage to remember cart contents, display preferences such as light or dark theme, and tutorial progress on the same browser and device. This prototype does not use local storage as a cross-device account system. Your browser may also store technical data according to its own settings.

4. Third-party services

Links to Instagram, TikTok, WhatsApp, Telegram and your email application lead to services operated by other providers. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies. If the website is later connected to analytics, hosting forms, payment or newsletter services, this policy should be updated to describe those services.

5. Data security and retention

We aim to handle messages responsibly, but no website or email transmission can be guaranteed completely secure. Information sent to us may remain in email systems for as long as needed to respond to the enquiry or manage related business records, subject to applicable requirements.

6. Your choices

You can choose not to submit optional information, clear your browser's local storage to remove locally saved cart and learning data, and contact us with questions about information you have sent.

7. Children

This website is not designed to collect personal information from children without appropriate parent or guardian involvement. A parent or guardian should supervise any order or contact submission made by a minor.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated as the website's features and services change. The date at the top will be revised when changes are made.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, email craftwearbynina@gmail.com or call +251 985 472 850.
