Learn at your own pace

Every stitch starts somewhere.

Follow a guided path from your first chain to confident, creative crochet. Complete lessons and pass each quiz to unlock the next level.

How progression works: Complete every lesson in a level, then pass its quiz with at least 70% to unlock the next level. Your progress is saved in this browser on this device. It does not sync between devices.

Learning note

These written lessons provide a structured starting point. Video lesson areas and demonstrations can be added by replacing the lesson resources with your own tutorial videos. Practice with safe, comfortable tools and take breaks as needed.

Advanced completion: The final level quiz acts as the advanced assessment. Passing it records completion of the learning pathway in this browser.
